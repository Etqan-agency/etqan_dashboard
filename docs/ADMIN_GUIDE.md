# ETQAN Dashboard — Admin Guide

What to enter in the dashboard once the website and dashboard are live on your domain, section by section, with the recommended values for ETQAN.

> **Golden rules**
> 1. **Real information only.** No invented numbers, clients, reviews, awards or results. If you can't prove it, don't publish it.
> 2. **English + Arabic together.** Every time you edit an English field, fill its **(Arabic)** twin in the same session. A blank Arabic field shows the site's built-in Arabic text, or hides the content on Arabic pages. It never shows English text there.
> 3. **Saving updates the website within seconds.** Each save refreshes the affected pages automatically. If something doesn't show, wait one minute (normal cache).
> 4. **Don't change slugs casually.** A slug is part of the page URL. Changing it is safe (a 301 redirect is created automatically), but do it only for a good reason, never repeatedly.

### Original Software case-study template (fill this first)

Interview the team for 30 minutes and answer:

- **Challenge:** Who buys software licences in Arabic, and what was hard before (trust, delivery, activation, Arabic catalogue)? 2–4 sentences.
- **Solution:** What did ETQAN design and build, and which decisions mattered (RTL storefront, 3-step buy → download → activate flow, brand identity)? 3–5 sentences.
- **What we built:** Keep the current bullets; add any missing features.
- **Results:** Only if measured: launch date, catalogue size, orders, speed. Or leave empty and add a client quote instead.
- **Gallery:** Catalogue page, product page, cart, checkout, activation screen, mobile view.
- **Live URL:** Only with the client's permission.
- **Before publishing:** confirm with the client the line "serving companies, banks and government institutions", or remove it.

## 6. Blog

Sidebar → **Blog**. Three bilingual articles are already there as **drafts** (not public):

1. How to choose a software development company: a practical checklist
2. Flutter vs React Native in 2026: how we choose for each project
3. 7 Arabic RTL UX mistakes that hurt conversions (and how to fix them)

**The Blog link appears in the website menu automatically once 3 articles are published.**

### Before publishing each draft

- [ ] Read the English and the Arabic version fully. Correct anything not true for ETQAN.
- [ ] Apply the review notes:
  - **Checklist article:** mention Egypt and the Gulf; link Original Software as proof.
  - **Flutter vs React Native:** make it about choosing for your business (cost, hiring, maintenance), not a developer tutorial.
  - **RTL article:** keep as is. It's the strongest one.
- [ ] Choose the **Author** (the founder).
- [ ] Add a **Cover image** (1600×900).
- [ ] Check **Meta title** (short, no "ETQAN") and **Meta description** (what the reader gets) in both languages.
- [ ] Tags: keep 2–3.
- [ ] Tick **Published** → Save.

### Writing new articles (editor tips)

- The body uses **Markdown**. Use the toolbar: **H2** for sections, **H3** for sub-sections, lists, links, quotes. **Never use H1**; the page title is already the H1.
- Click **Preview** to check. Arabic bodies preview right-to-left.
- Images: use the image button or paste an image. It uploads and inserts itself. Always write a description in the `![...]` part.
- Links to our pages must be **relative** and match the language:
  - English: `/services/mobile-app-development`, `/projects/tazakka`, `/contact`
  - Arabic: `/ar/services/mobile-app-development`, `/ar/projects/tazakka`, `/ar/contact`
- End every article with **one** call to action pointing to its service page or `/contact`.
- An article appears on the Arabic site **only if** both Arabic title and Arabic body are filled. Write Arabic only where Arabic readers search for the topic. It doesn't have to be a translation.
- **Planned next articles** (in order): Custom Arabic e-commerce store (guide) → Salla vs Zid vs custom store → App development cost in Egypt & Saudi Arabia (**needs your real price ranges**) → Replace spreadsheets with a custom dashboard → Website cost → Custom business software.

## 7. FAQs

Sidebar → **FAQs**. FAQs appear on each service page and help people decide before contacting you.

- **Service:** choose the service it belongs to (or none for a general FAQ).
- Write 4–6 **real** questions per service: the ones clients actually ask on calls. Answer in 2–4 honest sentences. English **and** Arabic.
- Use **Order** to control the sequence (lower = first). Tick **Active**.

Suggested questions (answers must be true for ETQAN):

| Service | Suggested questions |
|---|---|
| Web Development | How long does a company website take? · Can my team edit the content without a developer? · Will the site support Arabic and English? · Do you handle hosting and maintenance? |
| Mobile App Development | Do you build for iOS and Android? · React Native or Flutter — which will you use and why? · Do you publish the app to the stores? · What happens after launch? |
| Custom Software Development | Can you connect to our existing systems (store, accounting, payments)? · Who owns the code? · Can we start small and grow the system? |
| UI/UX Design & Branding | Do you design in Arabic from the start? · What do we receive at the end (files, design system)? · Can you redesign an existing app? |
| Digital Marketing | Do you only market products you built? · How do you measure results? · Do you manage ad budgets? |
| General (no service) | Do you work with clients outside Egypt? · How much does a project cost? · How do we start? |

## 11. Redirects

Sidebar → **Redirects**. Usually **automatic**: when a service, project or article slug changes, a 301 redirect is created for you.

Add one manually only when:
- you delete a page that had visitors: redirect it to the closest page, or
- someone shares a wrong link widely.

Rules: **Old path** and **New path** start with `/`, have no trailing slash and **no `/ar`** (Arabic is handled automatically). Keep **Permanent (301)** on. The **Hits** count shows whether a redirect is still used.

## 12. Messages (lead inbox)

Sidebar → **Messages**. Every contact-form submission arrives here (and by email).

**Statuses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New | Just arrived. Reply within the time you promised. |
| Qualified | Real project, fits our services and budget. |
| Proposal | Proposal sent. |
| Won / Lost | Final outcome. |
| Spam | Not a real enquiry. |

**Daily:** reply to every **New** lead (email, phone or the **WhatsApp** button in the lead), then change its status.

**Weekly (15 minutes):** review the week's leads: how many were **Qualified**, and where they came from (**Attribution** block: source, campaign, landing page). This is the main number that tells you whether the website works.

**WhatsApp and phone enquiries** that didn't come through the form don't appear here yet. Until a "log a lead manually" button is added, keep a simple note of them (name, date, source, status) so no lead is lost.

**Every year:** delete leads that **didn't become projects** and whose last contact was **more than 24 months ago**. The Privacy Policy promises this.

## 14. Images — sizes and formats

Use **WebP or JPG** (PNG only for logos). Keep files **under 500 KB** where possible. The website resizes automatically, but huge files still slow uploads. Maximum upload is 5 MB.

| Where | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Service image | 1600×1200 (4:3) | Photo or illustration; shown in colour-tinted style. |
| Project cover | 1600×900 (landscape) | Or a portrait app screenshot (e.g. 900×1600). |
| Project gallery | Original screenshot size | 3–6 images, with EN + AR captions. |
| Blog cover | 1600×900 | No text-heavy images. |
| Social share image | 1200×630 | |
| Author / team photo | 800×800 (square) | |
| Testimonial avatar | 400×400 | |
| Client logo | SVG or PNG, transparent background | With permission only. |

## 16. Never do this

- ❌ Invent numbers, clients, reviews, awards or testimonials.
- ❌ Present **team** projects (Tazakka, Hawadeet, DS Mate) as ETQAN client work.
- ❌ Paste English text into Arabic fields (or the other way round).
- ❌ Change slugs repeatedly, or reuse an old slug for a different page.
- ❌ Put "ETQAN" in meta titles (it's added automatically).
- ❌ Use H1 inside an article body.
- ❌ Publish an article without an author.
- ❌ Upload client screenshots or logos without permission.
